King is a playable character in Namco's _Tekken_, first released in Japanese arcades on 9 December 1994. He is a masked wrestler with a jaguar's head, and he fights using the throws, holds and submissions of professional wrestling. _Tekken_ itself is the work of a games company, not a wrestling promotion, which makes King a clear example of the registry's Homage & Influence criteria.

## The Homage

King draws on two wrestling sources:

- **Tiger Mask.** King's animal mask and his showmanship echo Tiger Mask, the masked hero of the 1968 manga who was later brought to life as a real wrestler in Japan. The charter itself names King as an example of a character homage to Tiger Mask.
- **Fray Tormenta.** King's backstory, a masked fighter who competes to support an orphanage, mirrors the real story of Fray Tormenta, a Mexican Catholic priest who wrestled under a mask to fund the orphanage he ran.

Sources differ on exactly which elements came from which inspiration, but they agree that King is a deliberate tribute to masked professional wrestlers. The original character was designed by Seiichi Ishii.

## Why It Matters

King's move set brought wrestling's distinctive throws, suplexes and submissions into a fighting game built mostly around martial arts. His popularity made the masked wrestler a familiar presence in 3D fighting games.

The character has remained part of the series for three decades: a successor wearing the same mask appeared in later games, and King returned in _Tekken 8_ in 2024. Through him, millions of players who may never have watched a wrestling match have learned its holds and its visual language.
