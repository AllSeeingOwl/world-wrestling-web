On 21 September 1933, Salvador Lutteroth held the first show of his new promotion, Empresa Mexicana de Lucha Libre (EMLL), at the Arena Modelo in Mexico City. Some sources give the date as 22 September. It is widely regarded as the beginning of organised professional wrestling in Mexico.

## The Context

Lutteroth had seen professional wrestling in the United States and set out to build a Mexican promotion, with financial backing from Francisco Ahumada. Unable to rent a larger venue, he used the smaller Arena Modelo, which became the first true home of lucha libre.

## Why It Matters

EMLL grew into the centre of Mexican wrestling. It developed the stars, styles and traditions that define lucha libre, including the masked heroes and villains whose identities were treated with great seriousness. El Santo, whose films are in this registry, became one of its biggest attractions.

The Arena Modelo site was later rebuilt as Arena México, still the home of the promotion and often called the cathedral of lucha libre. The company changed its name to Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre (CMLL) in the early 1990s, and it is recognised as the oldest professional wrestling promotion still in existence.

Its influence is visible in every lucha libre entry in this registry, from When Worlds Collide to _Nacho Libre_, and in the many luchadores who have since become stars around the world.
