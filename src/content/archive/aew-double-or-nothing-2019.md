On 25 May 2019, All Elite Wrestling (AEW) held its first event, _Double or Nothing_, at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas. About 11,000 people attended, and the show was broadcast on pay-per-view and streaming services.

## The Show

The card closed with three high-profile matches. Cody Rhodes defeated his brother Dustin in a bloody, emotional match; The Young Bucks wrestled Pentagón Jr. and Rey Fénix; and in the main event, Chris Jericho defeated Kenny Omega. After the main event, Jon Moxley, who had left WWE weeks earlier as Dean Ambrose, made a surprise debut, coming through the crowd to attack Jericho and Omega.

## The Context

AEW grew out of the success of All In, the independent event organised by Cody Rhodes and The Young Bucks in 2018, and was announced on 1 January 2019. Its backers promised an alternative to WWE, with more emphasis on in-ring competition and better treatment of wrestlers.

## Why It Matters

Double or Nothing showed that AEW could draw a national audience from its very first night. Moxley's arrival also signalled that established WWE stars were willing to join the new company.

Within months, AEW had a weekly national television show, and it became WWE's largest competitor in the United States since the closure of WCW in 2001. Competition between the two companies shaped the industry through the following years, including wrestlers' pay and the choices available to fans.
