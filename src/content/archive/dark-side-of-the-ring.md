_Dark Side of the Ring_ is a documentary series created by Evan Husney and Jason Eisener. It premiered on Vice TV (then Viceland) on 10 April 2019. Its first episode, "The Match Made in Heaven", told the story of Randy Savage and Miss Elizabeth.

This entry is for the series as a work of journalism, under the charter's Expanded Media criteria. It is not an entry for the events the series covers.

## The Series

Each episode examines one story from wrestling's past, often an event or a life that the industry itself had rarely discussed in public. The series is built on long interviews with wrestlers, family members, promoters and journalists, combined with archive footage and dramatised reconstructions.

Many of its subjects involve deaths, serious injuries and abuses of power, and several overlap with the kind of events that the charter reserves for the Cautionary Archive. The series has also returned to stories in this registry, including the Montreal Screwjob.

## Why It Matters

_Dark Side of the Ring_ showed that there was a large audience for serious, critical journalism about wrestling. It gave families and former wrestlers a platform to tell their own versions of events, and it encouraged a wider conversation about performer safety and wellbeing.

The series also reflects the purpose of this registry's Cautionary Archive: preserving the industry's failures so that their lessons are not forgotten. Not every subject has welcomed its portrayal, and some episodes have been criticised by people involved, which is part of the record of how wrestling's history is told.
