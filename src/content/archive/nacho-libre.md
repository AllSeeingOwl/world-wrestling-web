_Nacho Libre_ is a comedy film directed by Jared Hess and released in the United States by Paramount Pictures on 16 June 2006. Jack Black stars as Ignacio, a friar who cooks for a Mexican orphanage and secretly wrestles as the masked luchador "Nacho" to raise money for the children. Hess co-wrote the film with Jerusha Hess and Mike White.

## The Homage

The film is loosely based on the true story of Fray Tormenta ("Friar Storm"), the ring name of Sergio Gutiérrez Benítez. He was a Catholic priest who wrestled under a mask for 23 years to support the orphanage he ran. He makes a cameo appearance in the film as a retired wrestler.

Beyond its central story, the film draws directly on lucha libre's visual language: elaborate masks and costumes, tag-team partnerships, the importance of the mask to a luchador's identity, and the arena atmosphere of Mexican wrestling. It fits several of the charter's criteria for Homage & Influence, including narrative structure and aesthetic influence.

The same real-life priest also influenced the backstory of King, from _Tekken_, which is another entry in this registry. Fray Tormenta's story has inspired homages across both games and film.

## Why It Matters

_Nacho Libre_ brought lucha libre to a wide cinema audience at a time when it was rarely seen on mainstream screens in the United States. Its affectionate, if comic, portrait of the luchador as a working-class hero introduced many viewers to the traditions behind the masks, and it remains one of the best-known films about wrestling made outside the industry.
