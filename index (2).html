WrestleMania X-Seven: Stone Cold vs. The Rock

Date: April 1, 2001Promotion: World Wrestling Federation (WWF)

The main event of WrestleMania X-Seven, pitting Stone Cold Steve Austin against The Rock in a No Disqualification match for the WWF Championship, is widely considered the zenith of professional wrestling’s “Attitude Era.”

This match is historically significant as it definitively marked the end of the industry’s most lucrative and culturally ubiquitous period. The shocking conclusion, which saw the rebellious anti-hero Austin align with his long-time nemesis Mr. McMahon to secure the championship, fundamentally altered the narrative direction of the company and the broader industry landscape.

The bout itself remains a masterpiece of in-ring storytelling, crowd manipulation, and sheer dramatic spectacle.
