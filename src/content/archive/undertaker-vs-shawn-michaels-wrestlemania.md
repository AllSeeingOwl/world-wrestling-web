The Undertaker and Shawn Michaels met at two consecutive WrestleManias. The Undertaker won both matches, extending his undefeated WrestleMania record, and the second ended Michaels's full-time career.

## The Story

By 2009, The Undertaker had never lost at WrestleMania, and the streak had become the event's most famous tradition. Michaels, known as "Mr. WrestleMania" for his performances on the show, set out to end it. Their first match, at WrestleMania 25, was built on two veterans at the peak of their craft, and The Undertaker won.

Michaels became fixated on the rematch. The Undertaker agreed only if Michaels put his career on the line, and the match at WrestleMania XXVI was billed as Streak vs. Career. When The Undertaker won again, Michaels retired from full-time competition. He returned for only one further match, in 2018.

## Why It Matters

The two matches are widely ranked among the best in WrestleMania's history, and together they tell a story that neither tells alone: a challenger's obsession, a second chance, and a final cost. This is why they are nominated as a composite work.

The first match, at WrestleMania 25, stands on its own as one of the most admired matches of its era, and this entry proposes it for a Citation for Individual Merit. The second gives the pair its ending.
