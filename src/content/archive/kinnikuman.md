_Kinnikuman_ ("Muscle Man") is a manga by Yudetamago, the pen name of Takashi Shimada and Yoshinori Nakai. It ran in Shueisha's _Weekly Shōnen Jump_ from 1979 to 1987, and a 137-episode anime adaptation by Toei Animation began airing on Nippon Television on 3 April 1983. This entry is dated to the anime's premiere, the first date that can be confirmed precisely; the manga began in 1979.

## The Homage

The series began as a comedy about a clumsy superhero, but it soon turned into a story about wrestling, and that change helped make it a hit. Its characters, known as Chōjin ("superhumans"), fight in tournaments, form tag teams, switch between hero and villain, and finish their opponents with elaborate signature holds.

These are the narrative structures of professional wrestling, which the charter names directly: tournament arcs, dramatic rivalries, stable warfare and the "heel turn". The series also adopted wrestling's visual language of ring entrances, named finishing moves and over-the-top characters.

## Why It Matters

_Kinnikuman_ became one of the most popular manga of its era, with sales reported at more than 77 million copies in Japan, and it was revived in 2011.

Its influence reached far beyond Japan through toys. Bandai's small rubber figures of the characters were licensed by Mattel for North America in the mid-1980s and sold as M.U.S.C.L.E. ("Millions of Unusual Small Creatures Lurking Everywhere"), with a wrestling ring playset and a video game. Many children who collected them never knew the manga existed, but they played out wrestling matches with its characters at the height of the 1980s wrestling boom.
