# The World Wrestling Web Editorial Handbook

Welcome to the World Wrestling Web. This handbook outlines the editorial standards and guidelines for contributing to our professional wrestling registry. Our goal is to maintain a high-quality, historically accurate, and engaging archive of professional wrestling's rich history.

## 1. Core Principles

Our entries must uphold a standard of professionalism and accuracy.

*   **Historical Accuracy:** Entries must be factually accurate accounts of real professional wrestling events. Double-check all dates, locations, match outcomes, and participants. Do not present rumors or unverified backstage gossip as fact unless specifically documenting the historical impact of such rumors (and clearly citing them as such).
*   **Journalistic Tone:** Write like sports historians or respected wrestling journalists, not as fans (often referred to as "marks" in industry parlance). Avoid overly emotional language, extreme bias toward certain wrestlers, or "kayfabe" presentations (treating scripted storylines as literal reality, except when describing the narrative of the event itself). Maintain an objective, analytical perspective.
*   **Narrative Impact:** Focus on moments that changed wrestling. This includes significant debuts, major title reigns, legendary feuds, and poignant retirements. Ask yourself: "Why does this entry matter to the broader history of professional wrestling?"

## 2. Classification Rules

To ensure our archive is easily navigable and organized, entries must be classified appropriately.

*   **Era Classification:** Group entries by decade (e.g., 1980s, 1990s, 2000s, 2010s). For major shifts within a decade, you may use established historical eras (e.g., The Golden Era, The Monday Night Wars, The Attitude Era, The Ruthless Aggression Era) as secondary classifications.
*   **Promotion Categories:** Clearly identify the promotion(s) involved. Common categories include, but are not limited to: WWF/WWE, WCW, ECW, NWA, NJPW, AJPW, ROH, TNA/Impact, and AEW.
*   **Entry Types:**
    *   **Wrestler Profile Entries:** Focus on the career arc, significant achievements, evolution of the character/gimmick, and legacy of an individual performer or tag team.
    *   **Event Entries:** Focus on a specific match, pay-per-view, television segment, or historical incident. Detail the context leading up to the event, what occurred, and the aftermath.

## 3. Special Features

Certain types of historical moments require specific handling to fully capture their significance.

*   **Cross-Promotion Moments:** When a wrestler or event spans multiple promotions (e.g., the WCW/ECW Invasion, the NWA Worlds Heavyweight Championship being defended in TNA, or the "Forbidden Door" crossover events), clearly document the participating entities. Explain the business or narrative context that allowed the crossover to occur.
*   **Legacy Documentation:** For Hall of Fame inductees and major career milestones, provide a comprehensive overview. Detail the criteria for their induction, highlight the defining moments of their career, and assess their lasting influence on the industry and subsequent generations of performers.

## 4. Citation Requirements

Proper sourcing is essential for maintaining the credibility of the World Wrestling Web. All entries must include the following:

*   **Source Material:** Indicate where the information originates. This could be a specific television broadcast, a pay-per-view event, a recognized wrestling database (e.g., Cagematch, ProFightDB), an official promotion website, or a verified shoot interview/autobiography.
*   **Air Date / Event Date:** Provide the exact date (Month, Day, Year) the event occurred or was broadcast. If the event was taped prior to airing, provide both the taping date and the broadcast date if relevant.
*   **Promotion and Event Name:** Clearly state the promotion and the name of the show (e.g., "WWE WrestleMania X-Seven," "WCW Monday Nitro," "NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 12").
*   **Historical Context Statement:** Conclude or introduce the citation with a brief statement explaining *why* this source and this moment are historically significant (e.g., "This match unified the WWF and WCW Championships, effectively ending the Monday Night Wars storyline").
