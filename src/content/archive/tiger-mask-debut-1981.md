On 23 April 1981, at the Kuramae Kokugikan in Tokyo, a masked wrestler made his debut for New Japan Pro-Wrestling (NJPW) as Tiger Mask, and defeated the British wrestler Dynamite Kid. Under the mask was Satoru Sayama.

## The Context

Tiger Mask began as the hero of a manga created by Ikki Kajiwara in the late 1960s, about a masked wrestler, and was adapted into a popular anime. New Japan brought the character to life to attract younger fans. Some traditional fans were sceptical that a cartoon character could be taken seriously as a wrestler.

## The Debut

Sayama answered the doubts in the ring. His style mixed martial-arts kicks with fast, acrobatic moves that few audiences had seen, and his matches with Dynamite Kid were especially admired. Sources differ on whether the debut was also for a championship, so this entry treats it simply as the debut match.

## Why It Matters

Tiger Mask was a sensation with Japanese audiences, and the character became one of the most popular in the country's wrestling history. The fast, athletic style he helped popularise influenced junior heavyweight wrestling for decades afterwards.

The mask outlived its first wearer. Several later wrestlers took the Tiger Mask name, including Mitsuharu Misawa, whose 1994 match with Toshiaki Kawada is in this registry. The character also influenced King, from _Tekken_, another registry entry.
