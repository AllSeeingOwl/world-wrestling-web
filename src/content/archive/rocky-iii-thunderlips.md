_Rocky III_, written and directed by Sylvester Stallone, was released in the United States on 28 May 1982. Early in the film, the boxing champion Rocky Balboa takes part in a charity exhibition against Thunderlips, "the ultimate male", a giant professional wrestler played by Hulk Hogan.

## The Homage

The scene borrows directly from professional wrestling's world: Thunderlips' boastful character, his theatrical entrance, and a bout that turns into chaos as he throws Rocky around the ring and into the crowd. The exhibition ends without a winner, and the two part as friends.

It meets the charter's criteria for Homage & Influence through its use of a wrestling archetype, and through a direct parody of the gap between boxing and wrestling spectacle.

## Why It Matters

_Rocky III_ was one of the most successful films of its year, and Thunderlips put a wrestler in front of an enormous audience who had never watched the sport. Hogan's appearance reportedly cost him his job in the WWF at the time, as promoter Vincent J. McMahon disapproved of the role.

The exposure helped make Hogan a recognisable star outside wrestling. When he returned to the WWF in late 1983, that profile was part of what made him the centre of the national wrestling boom of the mid-1980s.
