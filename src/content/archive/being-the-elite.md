_Being The Elite_ is a YouTube series created by Matt and Nick Jackson, the tag team known as The Young Bucks. Its first episode was published on 5 May 2016. It is the registry's first entry for content created by wrestlers themselves, which the charter's Expanded Media criteria name directly.

## The Series

The series began as a behind-the-scenes video diary following The Young Bucks and Kenny Omega as they worked for New Japan Pro-Wrestling, Ring of Honor and independent promotions. Over time it mixed real travel footage with comedy sketches and storylines that played out only on YouTube, and Cody Rhodes became a regular member of the group.

## Why It Matters

At a time when most wrestlers' public images were controlled by the promotions they worked for, _Being The Elite_ let a group of wrestlers build and own a direct relationship with their fans. Its weekly episodes kept viewers following the group between matches, across several companies and countries.

That audience was put to the test with All In, an independently produced event in September 2018 near Chicago. Organised by Cody Rhodes and The Young Bucks, it sold out an arena of about 10,000 seats, which few believed wrestlers outside a major promotion could do. On 1 January 2019, the group announced All Elite Wrestling (AEW), which became WWE's largest competitor in the United States since the closure of WCW in 2001.

The series continued as part of AEW's world in the following years, showing how wrestler-made media could become an official part of a promotion.
