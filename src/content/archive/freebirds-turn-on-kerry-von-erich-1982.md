On Christmas night, 25 December 1982, at World Class Championship Wrestling's (WCCW) Christmas Star Wars show at Reunion Arena in Dallas, Kerry Von Erich challenged Ric Flair for the NWA World Heavyweight Championship in a steel cage. Michael Hayes of the Fabulous Freebirds was the special referee, and his partner Terry Gordy guarded the cage door.

## The Angle

The Freebirds had been presented as friends of the Von Erich family. During the match, Flair knocked Kerry into Hayes, and Gordy, apparently believing Kerry had shoved his partner, slammed the cage door on Kerry's head. Kerry was unable to continue, and Flair kept the title. Accounts of the exact sequence and finish vary, and one source dates the incident to a later show, but most place it on Christmas night 1982.

## Why It Matters

The betrayal turned the Freebirds into the most hated villains in Texas and began a feud with the Von Erichs that ran for about three years. World Class built its television and arena business around the rivalry, drawing large crowds in Dallas and beyond.

It is a model of how a long-running angle can be built from a single moment, using relationships the audience already trusted. The Freebirds, often credited as among the first wrestlers to use entrance music, also influenced how later wrestlers presented themselves.
