On the episode of _WCW Thunder_ broadcast on 26 April 2000, from Syracuse, New York, the actor David Arquette won the WCW World Heavyweight Championship. Some sources date the taping a day earlier.

## The Context

Arquette had become involved with World Championship Wrestling (WCW) through _Ready to Rumble_, a comedy film about wrestling fans that the company was helping to promote. By 2000, WCW was losing money and audiences, and was trying to win attention with shock storylines.

The title change came in a tag match: Arquette and Diamond Dallas Page against Jeff Jarrett and Eric Bischoff, with the stipulation that whoever scored the pinfall would become champion. Arquette pinned Bischoff to win the title.

## The Failure

This is a **Contextual Failure**. The decision may have been intended to draw mainstream publicity, but it misjudged WCW's audience. For fans, the world championship represented the best wrestler in the company, and giving it to an actor with no wrestling background told them the title, and the stories built around it, did not matter. Arquette has since said he did not want to win the title.

## Lessons for the Craft

The episode is now one of the most frequently cited examples of how WCW lost its audience in its final year. It shows that a championship's value depends on the audience believing in it, and that publicity which undermines that belief can cost more than it gains. WCW was sold to the WWF less than a year later.

Arquette later returned to wrestling and trained seriously as a performer, an effort documented in the film _You Cannot Kill David Arquette_.
