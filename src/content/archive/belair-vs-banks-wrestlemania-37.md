On 10 April 2021, Bianca Belair defeated Sasha Banks for the SmackDown Women's Championship in the main event of the first night of WrestleMania 37 in Tampa, Florida. It was the first WrestleMania singles match between two Black women, and only the second time women had main evented WrestleMania.

## The Context

WrestleMania 37 was WWE's first event with a paying crowd since the start of the COVID-19 pandemic, a year earlier. Banks was the established champion and one of the leading figures of WWE's women's division. Belair had won the women's Royal Rumble match earlier that year and was appearing at WrestleMania for the first time.

## Why It Matters

Both wrestlers spoke about the importance of the moment for Black women in wrestling and for fans who had rarely seen themselves represented at the top of the card. The match was widely praised, and Belair became one of WWE's leading stars.

It continued the path that began with matches such as Trish Stratus vs. Lita in 2004, also in this registry, of women's matches moving from the middle of the card to the top. Sasha Banks now wrestles as Mercedes Moné.
