_GLOW_ is a comedy-drama television series created by Liz Flahive and Carly Mensch for Netflix. Its first season was released on 23 June 2017. Set in Los Angeles in the 1980s, it follows a group of struggling actresses and performers who are recruited to star in a women's wrestling television show.

## The Homage

The series is loosely based on the real Gorgeous Ladies of Wrestling, a women's promotion whose syndicated television show ran from 1986 to 1990. Like the original, the fictional show is built on exaggerated characters, comedy sketches and in-ring storylines.

Much of the series is about learning the craft: the cast are shown being trained to take bumps, work together safely and build matches that tell a story. It uses wrestling's language of characters, heroes and villains to explore its characters' lives outside the ring. It meets the charter's criteria for Homage & Influence through its narrative and its close portrayal of how wrestling is made.

## Why It Matters

_GLOW_ introduced the history of women's wrestling to a large streaming audience, many of whom had never seen the original. It was widely praised by critics, and its cast trained to perform much of their own wrestling. Its release came as women's wrestling was gaining prominence in the major promotions, and it renewed interest in the original Gorgeous Ladies of Wrestling.

The series ran for three seasons. A fourth season was announced, but its cancellation was confirmed in October 2020, after production was disrupted by the COVID-19 pandemic.
