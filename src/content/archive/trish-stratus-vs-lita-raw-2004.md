On 6 December 2004, in Charlotte, North Carolina, Trish Stratus defended the WWE Women's Championship against Lita in the main event of _Monday Night Raw_. Lita won the title with a moonsault, ending Stratus's reign of 176 days. It was the first time a women's match had closed an episode of _Raw_.

## The Context

Stratus and Lita were the two most popular women in WWE in the early 2000s, and their rivalry had run for years, as allies and as opponents. By late 2004, Stratus was the arrogant champion and Lita the challenger, wrestling in front of a home-state crowd in North Carolina.

Women's matches on WWE television were usually short and placed in the middle of the card. Stratus has said that when they were told they would close the show, they thought at first they were being teased.

## Why It Matters

The match showed that a women's championship match could carry the most important position on WWE's flagship show. It took more than a decade for women's matches to main event regularly, and this match is often cited as an early landmark on that path.

Both wrestlers were later inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame: Stratus in 2013 and Lita in 2014.
